Kiến thức cần nhớ

1. Countable / Uncountable

a child → children | water, advice, bread
  • Countable: one book, two books, three children.
  • Uncountable: water, rice, information, money.
  • a piece of advice, two bottles of water.

2. Quantifiers

many / a few + plural C
much / a little + U
  • Some: khẳng định, lời mời / đề nghị.
  • Any: phủ định, câu hỏi thông thường.

3. Modal verbs

must / should / can + V
  • Mustn't: không được phép (cấm).
  • Don't / doesn't have to: không cần phải.
  • Have to / has to: bắt buộc, nghĩa vụ.

4. Quy tắc số nhiều

-s / -es / -ies / irregular
  • box → boxes, boy → boys, leaf → leaves.
  • sheep → sheep, woman → women.
  • photo → photos, roof → roofs.
